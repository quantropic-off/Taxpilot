Understanding GSTR-1: Table 4A

Section 4A of GSTR-1 is designed to capture Taxable outward supplies made to registered persons (B2B). This includes all supplies other than those attracting reverse charge and supplies made through e-commerce operators.

info Key Logic
  • • In Table 4A, the supplier pays the tax and takes credit (if applicable).
  • • Reverse charge supplies are strictly excluded here and reported in Table 4B.
  • • Amendments to these invoices are handled separately in Table 9.

Here's how the calculation logic is structured in the simulator engine:

08 }
09 return { igst, cgst, sgst };
